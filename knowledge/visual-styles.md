# Visual Styles ("Visual Cores") for Art Direction

Source: a Korean short shared 26 November 2026 ("Comment 스타일 and I'll share 56 visual cores"). It showed a Pinterest-style board of named aesthetics. These are the names that could be read on screen. The full board has 56.

## Why this matters

AI defaults to one look (see [anti-slop.md](anti-slop.md)). Naming a specific aesthetic in the prompt is the fastest way out. "Make it look good" gets you the purple gradient. "Vintage Japanese matchbox labels: flat inks, cream paper, red and teal, hand-set type" gets you something no competitor has.

Use these as prompt vocabulary and as review language. ("The brand says craft coffee. The site says SaaS dashboard. Try Apothecary Labels.")

## How to brief one

Name the style, then give 3 to 5 concrete traits, so the model doesn't guess:

```
Art direction: Swiss Style / International.
Strict grid, asymmetric layout, large flush-left sans headlines,
lots of white space, one red accent, photos cropped hard, no shadows or gradients.
```

Match the style to the brand's story, not to what's trending. A style that fights the product is still slop, just rarer slop.
